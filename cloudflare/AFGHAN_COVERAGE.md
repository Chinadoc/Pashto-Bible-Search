## 📊 Why Fewer Verses?

**Yousafzai 2019**: 30,410 verses (Full Bible - OT + NT)
**Afghan 2023**: 24,160 verses (Missing some OT books)

The difference (~6,250 verses) is mostly from:
- Missing Samuel books (1 & 2) - ~1,500 verses
- Missing Kings books (1 & 2) - ~1,500 verses  
- Missing Chronicles books (1 & 2) - ~1,700 verses
- Some variations in verse counts between translations

**Afghan 2023 is complete for NT and most OT books** - just missing those 6 historical books.
