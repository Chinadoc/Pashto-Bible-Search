# Quick Fix: Make Google Drive Files Public

## The Problem
- Audio files in Google Drive are **not publicly accessible**
- They return a sign-in page instead of audio
- There are **1,000+ files** to make public

## The Solution (5 minutes!)

### Step 1: Open Google Drive
Go to https://drive.google.com

### Step 2: Find Your Audio Folder
Look for the folder containing your Pashto Bible audio files

### Step 3: Make Folder Public
1. Right-click the folder
2. Click **"Share"**
3. Click **"Change to anyone with the link"**
4. Set permission to **"Viewer"**
5. Click **"Done"**

### Step 4: Test
The audio should now play inline! ✅

## Why This Works
- All files in the folder inherit public access
- No authentication needed
- Works immediately
- Free and easy
